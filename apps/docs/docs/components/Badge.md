# Badge

A short status word in a 1px box: `<span class="pd-badge">`.

**Consumer provides:** one or two words of status.

- The word carries the meaning. There are no coloured badges; a red "Late" would fail for anyone who cannot tell red.
- `data-tone="strong"` inverts it (`canvas` on `canvas-text`) for the one status per screen that most needs attention.
- Keep badge text uppercase and short. For a count, include the noun ("3 low", not "3").
