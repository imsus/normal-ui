# Help centre

The help centre home: a search box first, topics with their top articles, and a clear way to reach a person.

**Consumer provides:** topics, article counts and links, and contact details with hours.

- Search is the first thing on the page, with example queries in the placeholder and popular searches as links below.
- Topics are headings that link to the topic page, the article count in `gray-text`, and the two most read articles.
- Contact details are text people can select and copy, with opening hours and a typical reply time.
- Individual articles use the ArticlePage pattern.
