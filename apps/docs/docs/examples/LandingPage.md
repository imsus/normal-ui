# Landing

A marketing page in the system's plain style: a promise, one call to action, a real view of the product, three features, how it works and a closing call to action.

**Consumer provides:** the promise, features, steps and product view. Add figures only when they are real and sourced.

- The `h1` states what the product does for the reader in one sentence, up to 3rem.
- One primary call to action (`.cta`, `canvas` on `canvas-text`) repeated at the top and bottom; secondary actions are links.
- Show the product itself (a real table or screenshot with alt text), not an illustration.
- Features are three short blocks with a 3px `canvas-text` top rule; no icons or emoji needed.
- No carousels, autoplay video or pop-ups.
