Returning an item

Updated 28 September 2026 · 3 minute read

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Start a return from Your orders; you do not need to contact us first.

How to return an item

  1. Open Your orders and choose the order.
  2. Select Return items, pick the items and tell us why.
  3. Print the label, or show the QR code at any drop-off point.
  4. Keep the receipt until your refund arrives. It shows a tracking number like TR-2026-0419-88.

The returns policy covers sale items too:

Items bought at a reduced price are refunded at the price you paid, under the same return windows as full-price items.

Refunds

We refund to the way you paid within 5 working days of receiving the item. Bank transfers can take 2 more days to appear.

Questions

Do I pay for return delivery?

No. Every return label is free, whatever the reason for returning.

Can I exchange instead of returning?

Yes, for a different size or colour of the same item. Choose Exchange in step 2.

What if the item arrived damaged?

Choose Arrived damaged as the reason and add a photo. We refund as soon as you submit, before the parcel reaches us.
