Returning an item

Updated 28 September 2026 · 3 minute read

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Start from Your orders; you do not need to contact us first.

How to return an item

  1. Open Your orders and choose the order.
  2. Tap Return items, pick the items and tell us why.
  3. Show the QR code at any drop-off point, or print the label.
  4. Keep the receipt until your refund arrives.

The returns policy covers sale items too:

Items bought at a reduced price are refunded at the price you paid.

Questions

Do I pay for return delivery?

No. Every return label is free.

Can I exchange instead?

Yes, for another size or colour of the same item. Choose Exchange in step 2.

The item arrived damaged

Choose Arrived damaged and add a photo. We refund straight away.
