InputGroup: a field whose control is a row of joined members — `InputGroupInput` and `InputGroupSelect` that grow, `InputGroupAddon` text or icons in their own bordered boxes, and buttons that join by themselves. One member takes the visible label; two or more form a labelled group with per-member `label` props. Adds the shared `joined` primitive (`joined.item`, `joined.raised`, `useJoined`) that ButtonGroup, Button, MenuButton and Spinbutton now build on.
