# Success

The page after something important succeeds, such as placing an order.

**Consumer provides:** the confirmation, the reference, where a copy was sent, and the next steps.

- Lead with a success Alert, then a personal heading.
- Show the reference big and selectable in `--font-mono`, so it is easy to read out on the phone.
- List what happens next as numbered steps with rough times.
- End with the two most likely next actions as links. Do not auto-redirect.
