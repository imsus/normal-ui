# Pricing

Plans side by side, a full comparison table, and billing questions answered on the page.

**Consumer provides:** the plans, prices, limits and billing answers.

- Each plan is a `<section>` with its name as a heading, the price with "a month" in words, who it is for, and what it includes.
- Mark the recommended plan with a 3px border and a strong Badge; it gets the only primary call to action.
- The comparison is a real `<table>` with row headers, and "Yes"/"No" in words rather than ticks and crosses.
- The monthly/yearly switch is a radio group; say the saving in words.
- Answer billing questions with `<details>` right under the table.
